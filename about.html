About Us

Learn more about Juba Stationary and Printing

Our Story

Juba Stationary and Printing was established with a vision to provide high-quality office supplies and professional printing services to businesses and individuals in Juba, South Sudan.

Since our founding, we have been committed to delivering excellence in every product and service we offer. Our team of dedicated professionals works tirelessly to ensure customer satisfaction through quality products, fast turnaround times, and exceptional service.

Today, we are proud to be one of the leading suppliers of stationary and printing services in the region, serving schools, offices, government institutions, and individual customers with a wide range of products and services.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality office supplies and professional printing services while maintaining exceptional customer satisfaction through reliability, affordability, and excellence in service delivery.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of stationary and printing solutions in South Sudan, recognized for quality, reliability, and customer-centric service.
